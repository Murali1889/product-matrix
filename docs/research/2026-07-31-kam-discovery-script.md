# KAM/CSM Discovery — Interview Script

**Goal:** Find out which opportunity lever (Solutions / Convenience / Feelings)
is *actually* strongest for our revenue team, so we build the next thing against
a validated pain — not a guess.

**Framework we're testing:** every feature must sell via one of three —
1. **Solutions** (remove pain/risk), 2. **Convenience** (faster/easier),
3. **Feelings** (confidence, control, status).

**Who to talk to:** 5–8 people who own a book of accounts — mostly KAMs/CSMs,
plus 1–2 sales leaders for the top-down view. Pick a mix of tenures and
regions (India / ASEAN / Africa) so answers aren't skewed.

**Logistics:** 30 min each, 1:1, recorded (with consent). One person asks, one
notes. Don't demo the tool — we want their *current* reality, not reactions to
ours.

---

## How to run it (rules)

- **Ask about the last real instance, not the general case.** "Tell me about the
  *last time*…" beats "How do you usually…". Memory of a specific event is honest;
  generalizations are made up.
- **Shut up and listen.** After a question, wait. Silence pulls out the real answer.
- **Chase the pain, not the feature.** If they ask for a feature, ask "what would
  that let you do that you can't today?"
- **Never ask "would you use X?"** — hypotheticals lie. Ask what they *did*.

---

## 1. Warm-up (2 min)
- What's your role, and how many accounts do you own right now?
- Walk me through the first hour of your typical work day.

## 2. Meeting prep — reveals Convenience + Feelings (8 min)
- Tell me about the **last client meeting you prepped for**. Screen-share your
  actual steps if you can.
  - *Follow-ups:* Which tools did you open? What took the longest? Where did you
    have to stitch data together by hand?
- When you walked in, how prepared did you feel, 1–10? What would've made it a 10?

> Listening for: tool-switching pain (Convenience), "confidence/control" (Feelings).

## 3. Finding revenue — reveals Solutions (8 min)
- Tell me about the **last upsell or cross-sell you spotted**. How did you notice it?
- Was there one you **almost missed**? What would've helped you catch it sooner?
- How do you decide **which 3 accounts to focus on this week**? Walk me through it.

> Listening for: opportunity blindness, the manual detective work we could remove.

## 4. Risk & trust — reveals Solutions (5 min)
- When did you last realize an account was **ramping down / at risk**? How late did
  you find out? What was the cost of finding out late?
- What numbers in our current reporting do you **not fully trust**? Why?

> Listening for: churn blindness + trust pain.

## 5. The lever test — reveals what's REAL (5 min)
- If a tool that gave you [the thing they lit up about] **disappeared tomorrow**,
  what would you miss most? How would you cope?
- Complete the sentence: "I'd open this every single day if it just showed me ___."

> This is the money question. Whatever they'd genuinely miss = the lever to build on.

## 6. Wrap (2 min)
- If you could wave a wand and fix ONE thing about how you manage your accounts,
  what would it be?
- Who else should we talk to?

---

## Synthesis (after all interviews)

For each interview, tag every pain/desire to a lever:

| Lever | Signals to count | Strong if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Solutions** | opportunities missed, churn found late, distrust of numbers | pain is frequent AND costly |
| **Convenience** | tools opened per task, minutes lost stitching data | they lose >15 min on a routine task |
| **Feelings** | "unprepared", "anxious", "want to look sharp" | shows up unprompted, repeatedly |

**Decide:** the lever named most often *and* with the highest emotional charge
wins. Build the next feature against that. Set the success metric from Section 5
("I'd open this daily if it showed me ___") — that sentence is the spec.

**Rough test targets to set afterwards:**
- Convenience: time-to-answer drops >50%; ≥40% say "very disappointed if it went away."
- Solutions: N recommendations acted on / month; upsell win-rate lift.
- Feelings: they open it before meetings unprompted; NPS ≥ 8.
